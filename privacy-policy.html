FITBUDDY Privacy Policy

Effective: September 22, 2026

FITBUDDY is a fitness-planning and workout-tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Summary

  • FITBUDDY does not require an account.
  • FITBUDDY does not operate an advertising or analytics service.
  • We do not sell personal information or Health data.
  • Workout and profile information is stored locally on your devices.
  • Apple Health access and location access for Apple Weather are optional.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple; FITBUDDY does not receive payment-card details.

Information stored on your devices

FITBUDDY stores information you provide or create, including body weight entered in the app, onboarding acknowledgements, workout plans you create, custom exercises, completed workouts, repetitions, weights, durations, distances, rest settings, and progress. This information is stored locally using Apple-provided storage technologies. FITBUDDY does not operate a server that receives this information.

Apple Health and workout information

If you grant permission, FITBUDDY may read relevant information from Apple Health to provide fitness trends and workout context. Depending on the features you enable, this can include workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, heart-rate recovery, cardio fitness (VO₂ max), active energy, sleep information, and body weight.

If you choose to sync workouts, FITBUDDY may save completed workouts and active-energy estimates to Apple Health. Apple Health permissions are controlled by you and may be changed at any time in the Health app or device Settings. FITBUDDY does not use Health information for advertising, marketing, data mining, or sale, and does not send Health information to Apple Weather, Google, or other external websites.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange workout plans, workout progress, elapsed time, heart-rate readings, and active-energy estimates through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This exchange is between your paired Apple devices.

In-app purchases

If you purchase Watch Sync Lifetime, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. FITBUDDY receives the verified product identifier and entitlement status needed to unlock and restore the feature. FITBUDDY does not receive or store your payment-card number or other payment credentials. Apple handles purchase and transaction information under its own privacy terms.

Location and weather

If you enable local weather, FITBUDDY requests access to your current location while the app is in use. Depending on your iOS settings, the location supplied to the app may be precise or approximate. FITBUDDY provides that location to Apple Weather through WeatherKit solely to request current local weather. FITBUDDY does not persist your location or create a location history.

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. FITBUDDY displays the required Apple Weather mark and provides access to Apple's weather-data attribution page in the app. Apple's handling of information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

External websites

When you choose an exercise-help link, FITBUDDY opens your browser with a Google search query containing the exercise name and words requesting proper-form guidance. Some bundled exercise guides also contain optional links to their source on Wger or to applicable Creative Commons license information. Those websites handle information according to their own privacy policies. FITBUDDY does not perform a Google search or open one of these websites unless you select the corresponding link.

Sharing, advertising, and analytics

FITBUDDY does not include third-party advertising or behavioral-analytics SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is disclosed only when you direct the app to interact with Apple Health, Apple Weather, your paired Apple Watch, or an external website as described above, or when disclosure is required by law.

Apple may separately process App Store, TestFlight, purchase, crash, or diagnostic information as part of providing its platforms and services, subject to your device settings and Apple's own privacy terms. FITBUDDY does not receive that information through a developer-operated analytics service.

Your choices and permissions

You can decline or revoke Apple Health and location permissions through iOS Settings and the Health app. Disabling a permission may make its related feature unavailable but does not prevent you from using the app's local workout-planning and logging features. You may remove local workout information through available app controls or by deleting the app. Information already saved to Apple Health must be managed through Apple Health.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it through available app controls or remove the app. FITBUDDY does not provide a server-side account because it does not operate a server that stores your workout or profile information. Information saved to Apple Health is managed separately through Apple Health. Data handled by Apple Weather or an external website is subject to that service's retention rules. App Store purchase records and the entitlement used to restore Watch Sync are maintained by Apple under Apple's retention rules.

Security

FITBUDDY relies on Apple platform security and encrypted network connections where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

FITBUDDY is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a FITBUDDY-operated server.

Fitness and medical disclaimer

FITBUDDY provides general exercise reference information, tools for building and recording your own workouts, and estimates. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare or fitness professional.

You are responsible for choosing the exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, durations, and training schedule you record in FITBUDDY. Your choices may not be safe or suitable for your medical conditions, injuries, medications, disabilities, pregnancy, equipment limitations, or individual circumstances. Recovery percentages, calorie estimates, workout durations, heart trends, cardio fitness, and other insights may be incomplete or inaccurate and should not be used to make medical decisions or determine whether it is safe to exercise.

Exercise involves a risk of injury. Stop exercising if you experience pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, or feel unwell, and seek appropriate professional or emergency assistance.
